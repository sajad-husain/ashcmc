WHO WE ARE

Your Trusted Team of Cost & Management Accountants

ASH Corporate & Management Consultant is an Islamabad-based firm led by an experienced team of Cost and Management Accountants. Founded in 2021, our firm was built on a simple belief: professional, reliable, and affordable financial services should be within every business's reach.

From tax and corporate compliance to audit, accounting, and virtual CFO services, we partner with individuals, SMEs, and growing organisations to keep their numbers organised and their businesses profitable. We handle the complexities of regulation and reporting so you can focus on what you do best: running your business.

OUR EXPERTISE

What We Do Best

Our team combines deep technical knowledge of Pakistani tax and corporate law with hands-on industry experience. Our core areas of expertise include:

  • Tax Consultancy: NTN registration, tax planning, and return filing for individuals and corporations.
  • Corporate Services: SECP incorporation, WEBOC, PSEB, and PSW registrations.
  • Audit & Assurance: external and internal audits conducted to professional standards.
  • Accounting & Bookkeeping: accurate records, reconciliations, and payroll management.
  • Financial Analysis & Virtual CFO: insights, reporting, and strategic financial leadership.

Whether you are a startup registering for the first time or an established company looking to streamline your finances, our team is ready to help around the clock.
