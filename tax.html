Pakistan Tax Calculator 2025-2026

Salary Tax Calculator (2025-2026)

Salary — Key FBR Rules (Tax Year 2025-2026)

Rates (2025-2026)

  • Up to Rs. 600,0000%
  • Rs. 600,001 – 1,200,0002.5%
  • Rs. 1,200,001 – 2,200,00011%
  • Rs. 2,200,001 – 3,200,00023%
  • Rs. 3,200,001 – 4,100,00030%
  • Above Rs. 4,100,00035%

Key Conditions

  • Monthly salary is annualised (×12); monthly deduction = annual tax ÷ 12
  • 9% surcharge on tax if annual taxable salary exceeds Rs. 1 crore
  • Salaried status applies when salary is 75%+ of taxable income
  • Source: FBR First Schedule (Finance Act 2025)

Pension Tax Calculator (2025-2026)

Pension — Key FBR Rules (Tax Year 2025-2026)

Rates (2025-2026)

  • Government pension0%
  • Private pension, age 70+0%
  • Private pension, under 70 — up to Rs. 1 crore0%
  • Private pension, under 70 — excess above Rs. 1 crore5%

Key Conditions

  • Private pension (under 70): exempt up to Rs. 10,000,000; 5% final tax on the excess
  • Re-employed by the same employer or associate: normal salary slabs apply
  • Source: Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Section 12(2)(f) & Second Schedule

Business Tax Calculator (2025-2026)

Business — Key FBR Rules (Tax Year 2025-2026)

Rates (2025-2026)

  • Up to Rs. 600,0000%
  • Rs. 600,001 – 1,200,00015%
  • Rs. 1,200,001 – 1,600,00020%
  • Rs. 1,600,001 – 3,200,00030%
  • Rs. 3,200,001 – 5,600,00040%
  • Above Rs. 5,600,00045%

Key Conditions

  • 10% surcharge on tax if taxable income exceeds Rs. 1 crore
  • AOPs legally barred from incorporating (CAs, law firms) capped at 40% top rate
  • Source: FBR First Schedule, Part I, Division I

Rental Tax Calculator (2025-2026)

Rental — Key FBR Rules (Tax Year 2025-2026)

Rates (2025-2026)

  • Up to Rs. 300,0000%
  • Rs. 300,001 – 600,0005%
  • Rs. 600,001 – 2,000,00010%
  • Above Rs. 2,000,000 — individual25%
  • Company landlord — filer15%
  • Company landlord — non-filer30%

Key Conditions

  • Individual: automatic 20% repair deduction, then slabs applied to net rent (Net Rent = Gross Rent × 0.80)
  • Company landlord: flat withholding on gross rent — no 20% deduction (Sec. 155)
  • Tax is adjustable against the final return (Sec. 15)
  • Source: Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sections 15 & 155

Property Tax Calculator (2025-2026)

Property — Key FBR Rules (Tax Year 2025-2026)

Rates (2025-2026)

  • Sale / transfer (236C) — filer2.75%
  • Sale / transfer (236C) — non-filer11.5%
  • Purchase (236K) — filer1.25%
  • Purchase — non-filer, up to Rs. 5 crore10.5%
  • Purchase — non-filer, up to Rs. 10 crore14.5%
  • Purchase — non-filer, above Rs. 10 crore18.5%

Key Conditions

  • Non-filer purchase rates apply to non-filers only; filing a return lowers these rates
  • Charged on the higher of gross consideration or FBR/DC fair market value
  • Source: Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sections 236C & 236K, Part IV

Caution & Sources

Caution: All calculations on this page are made on the basis of the FBR (Government of Pakistan) Income Tax rules for Tax Year 2025-2026 — the First Schedule of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 as amended by the Finance Act 2025, plus related rules of Sections 12(2)(f), 15, 155, 236C, and 236K. Figures are estimates for guidance only and do not cover exemptions, credits, provincial taxes, or your personal circumstances. Please consult our team for filing and planning.

Sources: FBR First Schedule (Finance Act 2025/2026); Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sections 12(2)(f), 15, 155, 236C & 236K and the Second Schedule.
