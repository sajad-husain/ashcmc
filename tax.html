FBR Income Tax Rules — Tax Year 2025-2026

Brief summary of the rules our calculators use

Category Key Rates (2025-2026) Important Conditions
Salary 0% up to Rs. 6L; 2.5% on Rs. 6L–12L; 11% on Rs. 12L–22L; 23% on Rs. 22L–32L; 30% on Rs. 32L–41L; 35% above Rs. 41L Monthly salary is annualised (×12). 9% surcharge on tax if annual taxable salary exceeds Rs. 1 crore. Salaried status: salary ≥ 75% of taxable income.
Pension 0% up to Rs. 1 crore; 5% on the excess Government pension fully exempt. Private pension at age 70+ fully exempt. Re-employed by the same employer: normal salary slabs apply. (Sec. 12(2)(f))
Business 0% up to Rs. 6L; 15% on Rs. 6L–12L; 20% on Rs. 12L–16L; 30% on Rs. 16L–32L; 40% on Rs. 32L–56L; 45% above Rs. 56L 10% surcharge on tax if taxable income exceeds Rs. 1 crore. AOPs barred from incorporating (CAs, law firms) capped at 40% top rate. (First Schedule, Part I)
Rental 0% up to Rs. 3L; 5% on Rs. 3L–6L; 10% on Rs. 6L–20L; 25% above Rs. 20L (of net rent) Automatic 20% repair deduction for individuals (Net Rent = Gross Rent × 0.80). Company landlords: flat 15% (filer) / 30% (non-filer) withholding. Adjustable against final return. (Sec. 15 & 155)
Property Sale (236C): 2.75% filer / 11.5% non-filer. Purchase (236K): 1.25% filer; non-filers 10.5%–18.5% in bands Charged on the higher of gross consideration or FBR/DC fair market value. Non-filer purchase bands: 10.5% up to Rs. 5 crore, 14.5% up to Rs. 10 crore, 18.5% above. (Sec. 236C & 236K)

Caution: All calculations on this page are made on the basis of the FBR (Government of Pakistan) Income Tax rules for Tax Year 2025-2026 — the First Schedule of the Income Tax Ordinance 2001 as amended by the Finance Act 2025, plus related rules of Sections 12(2)(f), 15, 155, 236C, and 236K. Figures are estimates for guidance only and do not cover exemptions, credits, provincial taxes, or your personal circumstances. Please consult our team for filing and planning.

Sources: FBR First Schedule (Finance Act 2025/2026); Income Tax Ordinance 2001, Sections 12(2)(f), 15, 155, 236C & 236K and the Second Schedule.
